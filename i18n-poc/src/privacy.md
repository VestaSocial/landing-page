Vesta helps families carry the mental load together — a shared calendar, tasks, household coordination, and reminders. Keeping your family's information private is central to how we build. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, where it's stored, and the choices and rights you have.

In this policy, "Vesta", "we", and "us" refer to the team that operates the Vesta app and the vesta.social website. If you have any questions, email us at [hello@vesta.social](mailto:hello@vesta.social).

## Information we collect

**Account and profile.** When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture, and use them to create your Vesta account. We never see or store your Google password.

**Family and household information.** The people, households, and relationships you add — including "managed profiles" you create for family members (such as children) who don't have their own account. This can include names, roles, birthdays, and relationship labels you enter.

**Your schedule and tasks.** Events (titles, dates and times, locations, attendees, RSVPs), tasks (titles, due dates, assignees, completion status), any notes you add, and recurring-event rules.

**Household budget.** If you use the budget feature, the income, expenses, savings goals, and allowances you record.

**Notifications and devices.** Your notification preferences, the in-app notifications we generate for you, and a device token for each device so we can deliver push notifications.

**Connected Google Calendar.** Only if you choose to connect it — see [Google Calendar](#google-calendar-optional) below.

**Content you send to AI features.** When you use natural-language features — for example typing "dentist Tuesday 3pm" to create an event, or asking for scheduling insights — the relevant text and schedule details are processed to produce a suggestion you then confirm. See [AI features](#ai-features).

**Usage information.** Basic events about how features are used within the app, which help us understand what's working and improve Vesta. We keep this in our own database; we do not use third-party analytics trackers.

**Technical information.** Standard server logs — such as a request identifier, timestamps, and error information — needed to operate and secure the service.

## How we use your information

- To provide and operate Vesta — your calendar, tasks, household coordination, and reminders.
- To send the notifications you've asked for, such as reminders, a daily digest, and activity in your households.
- To power AI features that turn your words into structured tasks and events and surface helpful scheduling insights.
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems, and improve features.
- To communicate with you about the service, such as important changes or support.

## Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the features you sign up for.
- **Consent** — for optional integrations you switch on, such as Google Calendar sync. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** — to keep the service secure and to improve it, balanced against your rights and freedoms.

## Who we share information with

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. To run Vesta, we rely on a small set of trusted service providers ("sub-processors"):

- **DigitalOcean** — cloud hosting and databases. Your data is stored in the **European Union** (Amsterdam).
- **Google** — sign-in (verifying your Google identity) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications), and — only if you connect it — the Google Calendar API.
- **OpenAI** — processes the text you send to AI features to generate suggestions (see below).

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.

## Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google Calendar, Vesta requests access to your calendar events so it can show them alongside your Vesta schedule and, where you enable it, keep events in sync in both directions. We store the authorization for this in **encrypted** form and use it only to provide the calendar features you turned on. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which removes our access.

Vesta's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google Calendar data only to provide and improve the calendar features you enable — never to serve advertising — and we do not sell it or transfer it to others except as needed to provide the service.

## AI features

Some features use a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI) to interpret what you type. For example, when you enter a task or event in plain language, we send that text — and, for scheduling insights, the relevant details of your upcoming schedule — to the provider to generate a structured suggestion, which you then review and confirm. We share only what the feature needs. Under our agreement with the provider, this content is **not** used to train its models. If you would rather not use these features, you can always enter details manually.

## Children

Vesta is designed for adults — parents and caregivers coordinating family life. It is not directed to children, and children do not create their own Vesta accounts. Where information about a child appears in Vesta, it is entered and controlled by an adult in the household through a "managed profile." If you believe a child has provided us with information directly, contact us and we will remove it.

## Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Your data is hosted in the **European Union** (DigitalOcean, Amsterdam). We protect it with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, access controls, and additional encryption for particularly sensitive items such as your Google Calendar authorization. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information and to limit what we collect to what the app actually needs.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and family data for as long as your account is active. Notifications are automatically purged after a limited period. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, except where we are required to retain certain records by law. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data — including to **access** it, **correct** it, **delete** it, **export** it (portability), **object to** or **restrict** certain processing, and **withdraw consent** for optional features. To exercise any of these, email us at [hello@vesta.social](mailto:hello@vesta.social). If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## The vesta.social website

This website uses a privacy-friendly, EU-hosted web-font provider (Bunny Fonts) and an embedded waitlist form (Tally). It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If you submit the waitlist form, we use your email address only to contact you about Vesta.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Vesta evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
