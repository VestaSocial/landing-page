If you'd like to delete your Vesta account and the personal data associated with it, we'll take care of it — just ask.

## How to request deletion

Email **[hello@vesta.social](mailto:hello@vesta.social)** with the subject **"Delete my account."** Please send it **from the Google account you sign in to Vesta with**, so we can confirm the request is really you. We'll verify it, complete the deletion, and let you know when it's done.

## What gets deleted

When we delete your account, we remove your personal data from Vesta, including:

- your account and profile (name, email address, and Google profile picture);
- the people, households, and managed profiles you created;
- your events, tasks, notes, and RSVPs;
- your household budget entries;
- your notifications and device tokens; and
- any Google Calendar connection and the authorization stored for it.

## What may be kept

We may retain a limited amount of information where we are required to by law, and we may keep aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer be linked to you.

Note: information you shared into a household that other members still rely on — for example, an event you created that others are attending — may remain part of *their* records after your account is deleted.

## How long it takes

We process deletion requests and remove your personal data **within 30 days** of verifying your request.

## Questions

Any questions about deleting your account or your data? Email **[hello@vesta.social](mailto:hello@vesta.social)**. You can also read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for more on how we handle your data.
